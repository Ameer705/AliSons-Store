Need More Help?

General Questions

Everything you need to know before placing an order.

1. How do I place an order?

Browse our products, add your favorite items to the cart, proceed to checkout, fill in your delivery details, choose a payment method, and place your order.


2. What payment methods do you accept?

We currently support Cash on Delivery (COD), Mada, Visa, MasterCard, Apple Pay, and STC Pay.


3. Do you deliver across Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We provide delivery to customers throughout Saudi Arabia.


4. How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location. Most orders are delivered within a few business days.


5. Can I cancel my order?

Yes. Please contact our customer support as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order before it has been shipped.

Returns & Refunds

6. Can I return a product?

Yes. Eligible products can be returned according to our Return Policy.


7. What if I receive a damaged or incorrect item?

Contact our support team immediately with your order details and photos of the item. We'll work to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

Account & Orders

Helpful information about your account and orders.

8. Do I need an account to shop?

No. You can place an order as a guest, but creating an account allows you to track orders and save your wishlist.


9. How can I track my order?

After your order is confirmed, you'll receive updates about its status. You can also contact our support team for assistance.


10. Is my payment information secure?

Yes. We are committed to protecting your personal information and use secure checkout methods for all transactions.
