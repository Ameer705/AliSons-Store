Return Policy

Please review our return conditions before requesting a return.

Return Period

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order, provided the item meets our return requirements.


Eligible Items

Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and documentation.


Non-Returnable Items

Certain items such as personalized products, hygiene products, or items marked as non-returnable cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.


Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 24 hours and include photos so we can resolve the issue quickly.

Refund Policy

How refunds are processed at AliSons Store.

Refund Approval

After we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you whether your refund request has been approved.


Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–10 business days. The exact time depends on your payment provider or bank.


Shipping Costs

Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the return is due to an error on our part or you received a damaged or incorrect product.
